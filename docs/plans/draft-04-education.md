## Decisions

**Middle School Diploma — excluded from resume.**

The DPS Middle School Diploma (Apr 2019 – Apr 2020, 91%, EL-32) is
deliberately omitted from this resume draft. Per the master prompt's
explicit rule ("Do not allow high-school information to occupy valuable
space over professional experience, projects, technical skills, or
research" and "School-level education should only remain if space
permits"), this entry predates even high school and adds no signal beyond
what the High School line already conveys for a candidate with 4
internships and a publication.

This is a documented omission, not a missed fact: the claim remains
verified in the evidence ledger (EL-32) and is available for a future
version of the resume if space or context ever calls for it.
